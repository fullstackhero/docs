---
title: How to deploy to production
description: High-level guidance for deploying the FullStackHero starter kit to production environments.
---

Deployment details vary by platform, but this guide highlights the main considerations.

## Checklist

- Configure connection strings and secrets via environment variables.
- Enable production logging sinks and review health checks.
- Configure migrations and seeding for initial tenants.
- Lock down OpenAPI endpoints and administrative APIs.
