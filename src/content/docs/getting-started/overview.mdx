FullStackHero is an opinionated .NET 10 starter kit for building modular, multi-tenant SaaS applications and enterprise APIs. It gives you a cohesive solution layout, tenant-aware persistence, CQRS, logging, and cross-cutting infrastructure so you can focus on your domain instead of boilerplate.

## What you get

- **Modern .NET 10 stack** – Minimal APIs, Mediator-powered CQRS, EF Core, Serilog, health checks, rate limiting, and RFC7807 error responses.
- **Modular architecture** – Building blocks in `BuildingBlocks/*` plus feature-first vertical slices under `Modules.*`.
- **Multi-tenancy built-in** – Finbuckle.MultiTenant integration with tenant-aware DbContexts and migrations.
- **Production-friendly defaults** – Structured logging, HTTP auditing, OpenAPI + Scalar UI, and architecture tests.
